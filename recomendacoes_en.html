Analytical foundation
What the data says
5 proven facts — the foundation of every recommendation
1
Baron = result in 7/7 games. The correlation is perfect: RED won 6 Barons, corresponding to FURIA's 6 losses. FURIA's only Baron = their only win.
2
FURIA does not lose the early game. Avg GD@10 in losses: +56. The collapse happens between @15 and @20, from −141 to −1184 average gold. This is not an early game talent problem.
3
FURIA's vision outperforms RED in all 7 games, with a 1.2 to 1.65× margin in ward kills/min. The problem is not information, it is the decisions made with it.
4
Tatu beats Curse in all 7 games. RED wins despite this, their mid game system compensates for the weakest link in the league. FURIA has no equivalent system.
5
JoJo is the #1 support in the league (rating 8.04), but drops to 6.10 vs frosty, the largest contextual drop on the roster (−1.94). The cause is the pick matchup, not individual level.
Overall roster context

FURIA has all 5 players ranked top-5 in their respective roles in the league. The problem does not require a roster change. It requires building a system, because the gold advantage does not convert into Baron because there is no protocol to turn vision information into coordinated collective decision-making.

The 7 Recommendations
Short term · immediate action · Mid term · system building
01
Short TermVOD Review · Vision
Collective review of vision types, conventional wards vs passive sources
Responsibility: analyst + all players
⌄
Evidência
Playoffs G1: Tutsz moved through an area covered by the Scuttle Crab, a passive effect of the neutral objective, not a ward. He was picked off; the resulting teamfight was lost. JoJo had controlled conventional vision well up to that point. The loss was caused by disregarding a vision source that does not appear in VSPM or WK/min data.
G1: loss due to non-conventional vision
FURIA WK/min: 1.74 avg (outperforms RED)
O que fazer
In VOD review sessions, always dedicate time, even briefly, to identifying and naming every type of vision source active in games vs RED: conventional wards, control wards, Scuttle Crab vision, dead objective camera, map camera positioning. Build a shared vocabulary among all 5 players for in-game communication, so "hidden" vision sources never catch the team off guard. Coaching staff reinforcement is critical to ensure players respect the opponent and take the correct path, even when they are certain the enemy has no vision on their path. Yes, it is obvious, but that is precisely what makes this practice essential.
02
Short TermSystem · Baron
Explicit prerequisites protocol for starting Baron
Responsibility: head coach + shotcaller (Tatu)
⌄
Evidência
FURIA secured Baron in 1 of 7 games, the only one won. G3 showed the correct sequence is possible: JoJo physically contested vision → intercepted frosty → pickoff → Baron with numerical advantage. But it was the result of an individual read, not a defined protocol. LB PO G2: FURIA with +4,139 gold lead surrendered the Baron.
Baron FURIA: 1/7
Baron RED: 6/7
G3: only game with correct prerequisites
O que fazer
Formally define: the minimum vision state in the pit and access paths, who covers each quadrant, and the condition that authorizes starting Baron. Without a protocol, the correct sequence happens organically in roughly 1 in 4 playoff games, the data shows this. Another obvious point, but one that becomes even more critical precisely because of the low quality of mid/late game scrims in our region.
03
Short TermDraft · Preparation
Map and prioritize bans on frosty's space-control picks
Responsibility: draft analyst + head coach
⌄
Evidência
frosty is the 2nd best support in the league (rating 7.93), std dev 1.33, high consistency. His lowest rating across all matchups is 6.26 (PO-R1 G1) and 6.89 (LB PO G3), exactly the game where JoJo plays Alistar and physically contests the space. On Karma (G1), he pushed Rakan out of vision areas from range before any teamfight.
frosty overall rating: 7.93
frosty vs FURIA: 7.93 median
G3 (Alistar vs Bard): rating 6.89
O que fazer
Map frosty's full space-control pick pool and include at least one as a priority ban in games vs RED, especially Karma. The goal is not to eliminate frosty's impact, it is to force a pick that JoJo can physically contest in the mid game. JoJo is FURIA's hidden protagonist: his comfort level in the series is a decisive factor vs RED (and other chaotic/aggressive teams) that can easily go unnoticed, since his lower rating in these matchups hides an in-game difficulty behind his strong overall performance.
04
Mid TermTransition · Macro
Early → mid transition protocol: how to convert a positive GD@15 into map positioning
Responsibility: head coach + thematic scrims
⌄
Evidência
In losses vs RED: avg GD@10 = +56 → GD@15 = −141 → GD@20 = −1,184. The drop from +56 to −141 in 5 minutes is not coincidence, it is the moment RED executes their mid game system and FURIA has no structured response. FURIA has the resources to dominate this period; they lack the protocol.
Avg GD@10 (losses): +56
Avg GD@15 (losses): −141
Avg GD@20 (losses): −1,184
O que fazer
Thematic scrims focused specifically on the 14–20 min window: how lane advantages convert into map pressure before Baron spawns. Where to position each player, which objective to prioritize, how early game vision should drive mid game positioning. The success indicator will be visible in the data: the collapse between @15 and @20 will shrink. Another recommendation whose obviousness only increases the need for it to be done.
05
Mid TermKnowledge · Objectives
Include non-conventional vision sources in objective preparation
Responsibility: analyst + objective VOD review
⌄
Evidência
Playoffs G1: the Scuttle Crab vision is not a ward, it is a passive source created by the neutral objective that persists on the map. Tutsz did not recognize the coverage, moved through the area and was picked off. This type of information does not appear in vision statistics and is not captured by FURIA's ward kills (which outperform RED in every game).
G1: pickoff by Scuttle Crab vision
Not captured by WK/min
O que fazer
Continuing from recommendation 01, include in pre-game preparation a checklist of all active vision sources on the map beyond wards: Scuttle Crab camera, dead objective camera vision, objective unit vision. Small preparation cost, eliminates an entire category of loss.
06
Mid TermSystem · Mid game
Build a Baron system independent of lane state
Responsibility: head coach + conversion scrims
⌄
Evidência
RED won Baron with a GD@15 of −2,659, with Curse −1,421, fNb −1,248 and Kaze −1,013 in their lanes, three players negative. The system worked regardless. The clearest reference: LOUD has a 0.906 baron rate against RED while having the worst average GD@15 in the region (−1,505). Baron is independent of early game when the system exists.
RED Baron + GD@15 −2,659
LOUD BR vs RED: 0.906
FURIA BR vs RED: 0.143
O que fazer
Work specific Baron scenarios in scrims from neutral and deficit situations, not only when FURIA is ahead. Convert gold difference into map positioning proactively, regardless of each lane's state. The @15→@20 collapse will shrink once the system is consolidated.
07
Mid TermDevelopment · JoJo
Develop the team for better decision adaptation around JoJo's picks
Responsibility: lane coach + JoJo
⌄
Evidência
JoJo is the #1 support in the league (rating 8.04, std dev 1.71). His drop to 6.10 in matchups vs frosty (−1.94) is the largest contextual drop on the roster, with an identified cause: it is not a skill limitation, it is a pick pool limitation. On Alistar (G3): physically contested vision → intercepted frosty → pickoff → Baron → win. On Rakan, Nami and Milio: the vision contest was resolved by RED before any teamfights.
JoJo overall rating: 8.04 (#1 support in league)
JoJo vs frosty: 6.10 median (−1.94)
Alistar G3: frosty's only loss in the vision contest
O que fazer
Intentionally evaluate and develop JoJo's depth on engage tank supports (Alistar, Leona, Rell) so that option is available whenever the draft permits, not just occasionally. G3 proves JoJo executes correctly on this type of pick. JoJo is excellent on peel supports and that should continue to be leveraged, but it is necessary for him and the team to develop their vision control approach and how to play off it when he plays these picks, because objective setup roles shift dramatically from tank to peel, which once again (and I promise this is the last time I say this) is so obvious it can end up going unnoticed since everybody already knows this.

◉ Final Conclusion
FURIA already has the early game.
They need to build what comes after.

None of these recommendations require a roster change or an identity shift. The roster is elite, with all 5 players ranked top-5 in their respective roles. Tatu beat Curse in all 7 games. JoJo is the best support in the league. The problem is systemic: the gold advantage does not convert into Baron control because there is no protocol to turn vision information into coordinated collective decision-making. G3 proves FURIA can execute the correct system, and it needs to become the standard, not the exception.
